# BookNook SQL Analysis: Key Findings

*Data is synthetic (generated for this project). Revenue = completed orders only, in PKR.*

1. **Overall:** 2,914 orders were loaded; 2,620 were completed (PKR 6,644,850 revenue, about PKR 2,536 per order). 5.6% of orders were cancelled and 4.2% returned.
2. **Growth:** Monthly revenue rose from PKR 614,900 in April to PKR 1,832,250 in September. The only dip was June (-14.7%); August grew 55.9%.
3. **Genres:** Non-Fiction (22.0%), Fiction (17.5%) and Sci-Fi (14.0%) earn the most. Children's books sell the most units (1,054) but rank 4th in revenue (12.7%) because they are the cheapest.
4. **Top books:** "The Fragile Harbour" (Sci-Fi) is the top earner at PKR 240,000. The top 10 spans seven genres, so no single genre dominates.
5. **Cities:** Karachi (PKR 1.43M), Lahore (PKR 1.31M) and Islamabad (PKR 0.88M) lead on revenue. Quetta has the highest revenue per customer (PKR 16,117) but only 21 customers, so it is a small sample.
6. **Loyalty:** 493 repeat buyers (2+ orders) generate 90.7% of revenue. The 247 one-time buyers generate only 9.3%.
7. **Seasonality:** From July to August, Children's books grew 159.4% and Non-Fiction 106.2%, consistent with a back-to-school effect.

**Recommendation:** Stock up on Non-Fiction and Children's titles before August, and focus on turning one-time buyers into repeat buyers.
